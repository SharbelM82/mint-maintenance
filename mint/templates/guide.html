Guide & formulas

Taken from the Guide and Example sheet of the Mintresponse Work Order List.

Definitions

Direct cost
Total labor, subcontractor, material, travel and other direct job costs. Include parts cost here only once.
Parts ordered / status
Parts details or ordering status. This text does not add to direct cost.
Approved NTE
Client-approved maximum charge (Not To Exceed). It is not revenue. The NTE check flags a quote or invoice above this limit.
Quote
Direct cost × (1 + target markup %).
Invoice revenue
The actual invoice amount excluding sales tax, after discounts or credits. Do not enter the NTE as revenue.
Gross profit
Invoice revenue − direct cost. Gross margin = gross profit ÷ invoice revenue.
Actual markup
Gross profit ÷ direct cost. Markup uses cost as its denominator; margin uses revenue.
Net profit
Gross profit − allocated overhead − payment/admin fees − other expenses/tax. Enter all three expenses; use 0 when none.
Net margin
Net profit ÷ invoice revenue. Job-level profit after entered allocations, not company-wide net income.
Payment due
Invoice sent date + payment terms (days).
Balance due
Invoice revenue − amount received, both excluding sales tax. A negative balance means an overpayment.
Missing inputs
Results stay blank until their inputs are entered. Rates show n.a. if their denominator is zero.
Totals
Profit and balance totals add up calculated rows only. Resolve every “Enter…” status before relying on them.
Receivables aging
App addition: open balances on invoices that are sent (not Draft, Not invoiced or Void), grouped by days past the payment due date.
